What we collect

  • Game data stored on your device: progress, coins, settings, a random player ID and the leaderboard name you choose. This stays on your device unless you use online features.
  • Online features (optional, only when you are connected): your random player ID, display name, scores and run statistics are sent to our leaderboard server so scores can be ranked. We do not collect your real name, email or contacts.
  • Purchases: web/desktop purchases are handled by our payment provider (Stripe). We receive confirmation of the purchase linked to your random player ID, never your card details.

Advertising

The Android app shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID) and usage data to serve and measure ads, and to show personalised ads where permitted. Where required (EEA/UK) you are asked for consent through Google's consent form, and you can change your choice later. See Google's advertising policies. The web version may show Google AdSense ads. Rewarded ads are always optional.

Children

Pizza Stack is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

  • Play fully offline - nothing is sent when you are offline or when the online server is not configured.
  • Delete your data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling. To remove your leaderboard entry, email us with your player ID (Settings screen).
